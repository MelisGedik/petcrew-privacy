Pet Crew - Health Tracker: Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 25, 2026.

Pet Crew - Health Tracker is developed by Melis Gedik. For privacy questions or support, contact petcrewapp@gmail.com.

Records on your device

The current app works without an account. Pet profiles, appearance choices, birth dates, microchip IDs, vaccine and medicine records, vet visits, notes, care history, skipped doses, snoozes, weights, food records and language preferences are stored locally in the app's private storage. These records can include personal information you choose to write in names or notes. We do not receive these records on a developer-operated server.

The app does not currently include advertising, analytics, automatic crash reporting to the developer, account services or cloud synchronization. It does not request access to your location, contacts, camera or entire photo/file library.

Reminders

The app stores schedules on your device and asks for notification permission when you enable notifications. Optional precise-alarm access helps it schedule reminders more precisely; reminders can still work without that access, with possible delays. Android can reschedule reminders after a reboot or app update. Notification text may include your pet's name and care information; lock-screen visibility depends on your phone settings. You can change permissions and notification visibility in Android settings.

Backups and vet reports you export

You choose when to save or restore a backup, export a PDF vet report, or share a report with another app. Android's file picker provides access to the file or destination you select. The app does not automatically upload your records to us.

Backups contain your saved records and notes. Earlier builds allowed file attachments; any previously saved attachment copies may remain in your local records and backups, although adding or viewing attachments is unavailable in this version. Vet reports contain the records and optional notes you select. Backups and PDF reports are not password-protected by the app. Keep them somewhere private and share them only with people or services you choose.

If you select cloud storage, email or another app as an export destination, that service handles the file under its own privacy practices. Copies outside the app remain until you remove them separately. Sharing or opening a PDF may create temporary copies managed by the app, Android or the receiving service.

Retention and deletion

Your records remain on your device until you delete them, clear the app's storage or uninstall the app. You can delete individual pets and care records in the app. Deleting a pet also removes its linked care and weight records and cancels its reminders; food purchase history can remain separately. Restoring a backup replaces the current local library after your confirmation. Exported backups and reports are not deleted when you remove records or uninstall the app.

Save a backup before changing phones, clearing storage or uninstalling if you want to keep your records. We cannot recover local records or separately saved files for you because we do not hold a server copy.

Support correspondence

If you email support, your email address, message and any files you choose to send are received through the support email service and used to respond to your request. Do not send private pet or personal records unless needed for your request. You may contact the same address about deletion of your support correspondence, subject to any applicable retention obligations.

This privacy-policy website

This page is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub processes web request information, including IP addresses, to serve and protect its service. Opening this page does not send your pet records from the app. We have not added analytics, advertising, forms or tracking scripts to these pages. GitHub's privacy statement applies to its hosting service: GitHub Privacy Statement

Changes and contact

This policy describes the current local-storage version. If features such as accounts, cloud backup, advertising or analytics are introduced, the policy and required app disclosures will be updated to describe them. Contact Melis Gedik at petcrewapp@gmail.com with questions about this policy.
